# Enterprise Sales Performance Analytics Engine

## 1. Project Overview

Build a centralized enterprise sales analytics
platform using Python and PostgreSQL.

## 2. Business Objectives

- Track sales performance.
- Monitor quota attainment.
- Calculate incentive compensation.
- Analyze territory performance.
- Forecast future revenue.
- Generate business intelligence reports.

## 3. Functional Requirements

- Employee management
- Territory management
- Customer management
- Product management
- Sales transaction processing
- Sales target management
- Incentive plan management
- Commission calculation
- Performance analytics
- Revenue forecasting
- Reporting

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

- Scalability
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Data integrity
- Performance
- Security
- Testability
- Observability
